About Blessing Iyare Leadership Academy

Empowering Leaders Through Biblical Mentorship.

Our Story

Why BILA was founded: Born out of a divine burden to see spiritual leaders rise and take their place in the body of Christ and society.

The burden behind the ministry: A deep conviction that mentorship is the missing key to raising generationally impactful leaders.

  • Who it serves: Believers, ministers, and emerging leaders seeking spiritual and leadership growth.
  • The impact it hopes to make: To see transformed lives, churches, communities, and nations through biblical mentorship.

Vision & Mission

Vision

Empowering generations of spiritual leaders through transformative mentorship, fostering a community of disciples who inspire, equip, and release others into their God-given purpose.

Mission

To provide comprehensive mentorship training guided by biblical principles, equipping individuals to become effective spiritual leaders, mentors, and agents of transformation.

Mentorship PHILOSOPHY

BILA Manifesto

“We believe in the transformative power of mentorship, Empowering leaders to rise, equip and release others. With faith, humility, and servant hearts, We commit to guiding, supporting and inspiring, For the advancement of God's kingdom, Through the multiplication of disciples.”

Leadership Team

Blessing Iyare

Founder & Lead Mentor

Prof. Julius Iyare is a seasoned teacher of the Word, a respected voice in Christian leadership, and a passionate advocate for integrity in ministry.

With decades of experience in pastoral oversight, theological instruction, and ministerial mentorship, he has become a trusted guide to many across denominational lines.

Prof. Iyare’s ministry is marked by clarity, depth, and a strong emphasis on spiritual order.

He has served in various capacities—as a conference speaker, Bible college lecturer, and church planter—always with a heart to see the Body of Christ walk in truth, honour, and divine alignment.

He holds a Doctorate Degree in Human Resource Management from Leaders Esteem Christian University, USA, and was conferred the title of Professor of Biblical Education by All nation Bible Institution and Project, South Africa—a recognition of his lifelong commitment to raising leaders and reforming ministry culture.

He is the founder of Dream City Christian Centre Int., Inc., a thriving apostolic hub committed to spiritual growth and kingdom impact. He serves as the Chancellor of Apostolos Bible University, where ministers are trained with sound doctrine and spiritual depth. He is also the President of Blessing Iyare Leadership Academy, a platform dedicated to mentoring emerging leaders with excellence and purpose. In addition, he is the founder of Billa Ministers Fellowship, a covenant network that equips and empowers ministers through fellowship, accountability, and spiritual impartation.
Prof. Iyare is a prolific author, having written well over 72 books to date—each one reflecting his deep commitment to biblical truth, leadership development, and ministerial ethics. His writings continue to shape pastors, inspire believers, and strengthen churches across nations.

His ministry has taken him across West Africa, East Africa, and Central Africa, where he has ministered in conferences, trained leaders, and built lasting relationships with churches and ministries. His international reach reflects a global mandate to raise leaders and reform the Body of Christ. His academic background is matched by his spiritual insight, making him a rare blend of scholar and shepherd. Through his writings, teachings, and leadership, he continues to equip pastors, empower ministers, and strengthen churches with timeless principles rooted in scripture.

Prof. Iyare resides in Benin City, Nigeria, where he leads and mentors with grace, wisdom, and unwavering commitment to the gospel of Jesus Christ.
