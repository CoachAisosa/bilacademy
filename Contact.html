Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join BILA?

BILA is open to all believers, ministers, and emerging leaders who desire to grow spiritually and develop their leadership capacity.

Is membership free?

Yes, basic membership is free. Some specialized programs may have a nominal fee to cover resources and materials.

Do you hold online mentorship?

Yes, we offer both in-person and online mentorship programs to accommodate participants from all over the world.

How do I become a mentor?

To become a mentor, you must complete our leadership training program and be recommended by a senior mentor. Contact us for more details.
